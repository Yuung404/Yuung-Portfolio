CONTEXT & GOALS

Skybazaar (formerly Komptoir, then Fly Store) is my online store: a general store selling already popular products, with a more polished presentation than the big marketplaces. It runs on dropshipping: the supplier ships straight to the customer, so I buy no stock in advance. The goal: find a few winning products and promote them with video ads on TikTok and Instagram.

WHAT I BUILT

1. Market research
I analysed Amazon best sellers with AMZScout estimates, ruling out big-brand products that cannot be resold. For Black Friday, I picked 20 seasonal products (gifts, winter, wellness, pets) under €40.

2. Live store
Store renamed Skybazaar, custom theme published, SEO (titles and descriptions for Google), menu, 8 collections and 8 published pages: legal notice, terms of sale, shipping, returns, right of withdrawal, FAQ, contact, about.

3. Automated catalogue
The 20 Black Friday products were created through the Shopify Admin GraphQL API: photos imported from the supplier, variants, descriptions, SEO, and the CJ Dropshipping product ID stored on each product to place orders. A "Black Friday" collection fills itself from a tag.

4. AI video ads
15 videos in TikTok format: 10 product videos, then 5 brand-quality 14-second ads. Start frames are generated with Nano Banana from real product photos, animated with MiniMax, then edited by a program I wrote: animated hook, zooms, selling points appearing one by one and an end card with the price. The frame-by-frame render is encoded to MP4 right in the browser (WebCodecs), for an exact duration.

5. Sales channels and legal framework
Google and YouTube, TikTok, Facebook and Instagram apps installed to sell and advertise on those networks. Legal side: micro-business registration on the INPI one-stop shop (SIREN number obtained, awaiting validation), mandatory pages and honest reference prices for Black Friday.
