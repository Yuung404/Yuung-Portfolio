CONTEXT & GOALS

Komptoir (working name) is my online store: a general store selling already popular products, with a more polished presentation than the big marketplaces. I start from Amazon's familiar structure and rework it: typography, product layout, rating display.

WHAT I BUILT

1. Market research
Since Amazon does not publish sales figures, I used AMZScout estimates of the best sellers (category, price, rating, 30-day sales). I ruled out big-brand products, which cannot be resold without being an authorised distributor.

2. Interactive mock-up
A home page ranking the top 20 products with category filters, and a product page with quantity and cart. Bricolage Grotesque and DM Sans fonts, rating badge, visible rank. Mobile first.

3. Shopify catalogue
5 product pages created in the store as drafts until purchase prices and sample photos are ready: candle warmer, cat water fountain, under-sink mat, satin heatless curlers, heated throw (€29.90 to €59.90), with SKUs and ad-test tags.

4. Legal framework
Micro-business registration and consumer mediator, mandatory pages (legal notice, terms of sale, 14-day withdrawal), honest reference prices for Black Friday, no fake reviews, EU General Product Safety Regulation (GPSR), new customs fees on non-EU parcels.

5. Launch plan
Foundations, sourcing, store, ad content, then a deliberately small test of €25 per day per product for 3 days, with sorting rules applied every morning.
