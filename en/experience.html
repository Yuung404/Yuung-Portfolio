THE COMPANY

Groupe Bayard is the 5th largest press group in France, founded in 1873 and run by the Augustinians of the Assumption. A major player in Catholic and family media, the group publishes well-known titles such as La Croix, Le Pèlerin, Astrapi and Okapi.

With over 200 websites to operate and approximately 1,950 employees, Groupe Bayard's IT department ensures the digital continuity of a vast and critical media ecosystem. The Cybersecurity team, within the IT department, is responsible for protecting the group's infrastructure, data and digital services.
