CONTEXT & GOALS

SPOUDAZO Traiteur, founded in 2010 in Kinshasa, caters for ministries, embassies, international organisations and companies, and runs a Bar & Restaurant at the FIKIN. The client gave me a specification in July 2026: a showcase website presenting the company, its services, its menu and its references, with an online quote request.

The client's three priorities: security, easy maintenance and room to grow.

WHAT I BUILT

1. A justified technical choice
I compared WordPress (exposed through plugins and its login page), a hand-written HTML site (not scalable) and Wix or Squarespace (subscription, little control). I chose Next.js with static generation: with no database or exposed server behind the pages, SQL injection and admin takeover have nothing to target.

2. Centralised content
11 menu categories and over 100 priced items, 9 catering packages, 5 reference categories. Everything lives in 4 data files (src/data/), separate from the display code: changing a price is a one-line edit.

3. Design in three versions
After two rounds of client feedback ("too bland", then "not classy enough"), I rebuilt the site around a fine-dining look: deep black, gold, Playfair Display, full-width photos. 10 animations, all disabled for motion-sensitive users. A drawn illustration under every photo means no empty area on a slow network.

4. Customer reviews and QR code
Added beyond the specification: customers scan a QR code on the table and leave a rating. The API enforces 7 checks (mandatory moderation, bot trap, minimum delay, hourly and daily limits, HTML and link stripping), and IP addresses are only stored as a salted SHA-256 hash. Supabase database with row-level security.

5. Security
Six verified HTTP headers: Content-Security-Policy, Strict-Transport-Security, X-Frame-Options: DENY, X-Content-Type-Options, Referrer-Policy and Permissions-Policy. I also declined to publish the requested bank details: a copied page with another account number could divert payments.

6. Documentation
8 documents for the client and any future developer: project file with architecture decisions, changelog, security, hosting, maintenance, reviews and QR codes, a jargon-free go-live guide, progress status.
